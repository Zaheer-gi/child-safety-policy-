Child Safety Standards

App: Friendly Talk  ·  Developer: invoziatech

Friendly Talk has a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any such content or behaviour is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate removal, a permanent ban, and a report to the appropriate authorities.

1. Scope

These standards apply to every user, every account, and all content shared on or through the Friendly Talk mobile application published by invoziatech, including profiles, messages, images, videos, comments, and any other user-generated content.

2. Prohibited content and behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited on Friendly Talk:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or linking to Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, animated, or AI-generated material.
  • Sexualising minors in any form, including sexualised comments, images, or role-play involving anyone under 18.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a minor for the purpose of sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Sextortion: threatening or coercing a minor to obtain sexual images, money, or other favours.
  • Trafficking, solicitation, or advertising of minors for sexual purposes.
  • Any other content or conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

3. In-app reporting

Users can report any user, profile, message, or content they believe violates these standards directly inside the app:

  • In the app, go to the Report option available on user profiles and messages.
  • Users can also email our Child Safety contact listed below.

Every report is reviewed. Reports involving child safety are given the highest priority.

4. How we enforce these standards

  • We remove violating content as soon as we become aware of it.
  • We permanently ban accounts involved in CSAE and take steps to prevent them from returning.
  • We preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.
  • We regularly review our moderation practices to keep children safe.

5. Legal compliance and reporting to authorities

invoziatech complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations. When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM on our platform, we take appropriate action, which includes reporting it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant regional or national authorities. In India, this includes reporting through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and cooperating with law enforcement, as required under applicable law, including the POCSO Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

6. Child Safety point of contact

Our designated contact can answer questions about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance. This contact is available to Google Play and law enforcement agencies:

  • Name / Team: invoziatech Trust & Safety Team
  • Email: support@invoziatech.site

7. If a child is in immediate danger

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services right away. In India, call 112 or the Childline helpline 1098.
